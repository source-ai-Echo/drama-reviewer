## Failures found and corrected

### 1. Proposed additions appeared as existing facts

The first sparse-premise run placed a funeral deadline, evidence destruction, and personal danger directly into the story-engine summary even though the prompt did not establish them.

**Correction:** `SKILL.md` now separates supplied facts, interpretations, and revision possibilities. Missing engine fields must be marked as not established.

### 2. A summary action was expanded into assumed beats

The first anti-climax run expanded "prepares all night" into specific actions such as revising drafts and recalling memories.

**Correction:** `SKILL.md` now prohibits turning broad summary verbs into existing scene beats. Concrete sub-actions must be presented as optional examples.

### 3. Quick scan was longer than intended

The first quick scan returned three strengths and three priority issues.

**Correction:** the default is now no more than two strengths and two priority notes unless an additional issue is necessary for coherence.

## Remaining limitations
